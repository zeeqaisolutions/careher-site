Privacy policy

Effective November 17, 2026

CareHer is made by Zeeq AI Solutions LLC (“we”). This policy explains what the app does with information.

In short: CareHer collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. What you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app keeps, on your phone only

  • Your PIN, stored only as a scrambled (salted hash) value, never the digits.
  • Day 1 dates, only if you choose to keep dates. You can turn this off at any time, which deletes them.
  • Your cycle length setting.
  • What she told you today. It is replaced each day; no history is kept.
  • Which days you tapped “I did this”.
  • Your settings: Face ID on or off, lock behaviour, reminder time, and which app icon you chose.

All of this is stored in the iOS Keychain on your iPhone, set to “this device only”. It is not included in iCloud or device backups, and it is not transferred to a new phone. The full list is always visible in the app under More → What this keeps.

What we collect

Nothing. CareHer makes no network requests. We cannot see, access or recover anything you enter.

Face ID

If you turn on Face ID, iOS handles it. CareHer only learns whether unlocking succeeded. It never sees your face data.

Notifications

The daily reminder is off unless you turn it on, and iOS asks your permission first. It is scheduled on your phone, not sent from a server, and it only says “Today's move is ready.”

Deleting your data

In the app, go to More → Start over, or delete the app. Either removes everything.

Children

CareHer is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
