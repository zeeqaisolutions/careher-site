Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026 (version 1.1)

Care4Her is made by Zeeq AI Solutions LLC (“we”). This policy explains what the app does with information.

In short: Care4Her collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. What you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app keeps, on your phone only

  • Your PIN, stored only as a scrambled (salted hash) value, never the digits.
  • Day 1 dates, only if you choose to keep dates. You can turn this off at any time, which deletes them.
  • Your cycle length setting.
  • What she told you today. It is replaced each day; no history is kept.
  • Which days you tapped “I did this”.
  • Moments you add: a few words for each date (for example “Her birthday”), the date, your plan for it, and any notes you write about her for planning. One-off dates are deleted 30 days after they pass.
  • If you turn on Apple Intelligence: the short facts it picked out of your notes (for example a name or a food). You can remove any of them, and turning Apple Intelligence off deletes them all.
  • Your settings: Face ID on or off, lock behaviour, reminder and alert times, and which app icon you chose.

All of this is stored in the iOS Keychain on your iPhone, set to “this device only”. It is not included in iCloud or device backups, and it is not transferred to a new phone. The full list is always visible in the app under Settings → What this keeps.

What we collect

Nothing. Care4Her makes no network requests. We cannot see, access or recover anything you enter.

Apple Intelligence

Apple Intelligence is off until you turn it on. When it is on, Care4Her uses Apple's on-device model to turn a sentence you type into a date, and to pick facts out of your notes. This runs on your iPhone's own chip and works without an internet connection. Nothing you type is sent to us, to Apple, or to anyone else. Care4Her never uses Apple's cloud models.

Face ID

If you turn on Face ID, iOS handles it. Care4Her only learns whether unlocking succeeded. It never sees your face data.

Notifications

Notifications are off unless you turn them on, and iOS asks your permission first. They are scheduled on your phone, not sent from a server. The daily reminder only says “Today's move is ready.” Date alerts arrive at most once a day and only say things like “2 things to do today”, unless you turn on “Say what's due”, which names the step and the date on your lock screen.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Start over, or delete the app. Either removes everything.

Children

Care4Her is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
